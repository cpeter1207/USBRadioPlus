Safe control-plane ownership around the PortAudio/ALSA adapter descriptor.

The product radio session supplies separate bounded receive and transmit workers. This crate owns descriptor validation, USB selection, stream lifecycle, timing, statistics, and CM119 mixer handles without exposing PortAudio or ALSA types to the adapter-neutral radio core.
