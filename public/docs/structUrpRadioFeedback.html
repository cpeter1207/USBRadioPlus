Detailed Description

Immutable prefix borrowed immediately before the serial controller TX call. Positions refer to completed radio renders, not DAC or measured RF time. GPIO completed writes remain service evidence; offline/default false never becomes observed OFF. Acquisition age advances without refreshing cached facts. Transmitter state is the existing 0/1/2/4/5 state after the preceding render; fine STE, receiver settling and rekey completion are not measured here. Consumers require matching lifecycle/request identities and their own freshness policy before interpreting service or readback as current-request evidence.
