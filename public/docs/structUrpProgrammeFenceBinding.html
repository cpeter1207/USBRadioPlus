Detailed Description

Writable sized prefix shared by descriptor binds and ast_channel_setoption. Require zero return AND accepted_abi_version=1. Bind/rebind/unbind only after confirmed callback quiescence. Successful stop retains the binding for restart; failed start/stop does not authorize releasing it. Context/code remain live through stopped unbind/rebind or quiescent destruction/module unload. A missing capability cannot qualify private programme. An absent binding preserves ordinary PCM behavior; it is not an implicit private-media permit.
