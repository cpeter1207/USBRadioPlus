◆ native_programme_retire

int(* UrpAstDescriptor::native_programme_retire) (void *native, uint64_t expected_radio_generation, uint64_t request_id)

Explicit final-TX programme retirement on the active serialized native owner. expected_radio_generation must match the immutable physical session, including on repeated polls; replaced/unknown generations fail before preparation. Nonzero IDs increase monotonically within a physical station lifetime. Repeat the same ID to poll one transaction: RELOAD_PENDING retains all ownership; zero acknowledges a committed fresh TX graph with no predecessor crossfade or rollback to revoked programme. Prior/superseded IDs fail. Ordinary updates and remote DSP work serialize with this request. RX/RNNoise, signaling, hardware gains and ordinary unkey behavior remain unchanged. The caller gates programme until matching completed-render feedback; no new callback is bound.
